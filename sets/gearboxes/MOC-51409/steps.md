# MOC-51409 - 8-Speed Gearbox with 2 Levers (by TechnicBrickPower)

Booklet: booklet.pdf (93 pages: 88 build steps + 4 closing parts-legend pages). Fan MOC - parts list is
rebuilt from the booklet's own closing legend (not the pasted Rebrickable data, which disagreed on several
quantities). Joins are read from the picture like any other labeling pass, so `~` is used for hole
positions not shown clearly, and many small similar-looking connector/pin parts are a best reading of
the render rather than a certain match - this is the largest and least certain set in the project so far.
