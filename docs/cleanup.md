# Merge and cleanup, 2026-10-25

The overnight Technic work and newer main-branch MOC additions were combined. The separate `origin/42129-parts-booklet` branch was merged with its training and expanded inventory. Historical annotation branches contain retired per-step formats, already superseded by the current training workflow; they were reviewed rather than copied over current data. No branches or history were deleted or force-pushed.

All 60 set inventories now have local, tracked booklet PDFs. Existing local PDFs were copied without changing their bytes, and duplicate numbered copies were consolidated within each set. Missing modern manuals were downloaded from LEGO's CDN. For 8862 and 8880, the numbered JPEG page scans from Brick Instructions were assembled in order into PDFs without cropping or recompressing the source images. The Zetros branch's compressed 384-page reference is kept alongside the official 388-page edition. Source coverage and SHA256 hashes are in `booklets.csv`; older local files have local-source names where their original download URL was unavailable.

All 29 training pairs now share the 42175 header and chat schema, consecutive global steps, one WHY line and matching previous-step context. Existing build content and uncertainty markers were retained. 8047's embedded side builds were expanded into the same 88-step order already present in its legacy JSON, including six ADD records omitted from its old table. Its active source joins, gear lines and relationship statements were retained. MOC-183729's legacy explanations were converted to WHY lines. The invalid 42114 JSONL placeholder was rebuilt from its existing 54-step table. Two Zetros joins and six 8047 joins were changed from STIFF to TURNS because the existing catalog classifies their pins as frictionless.

Retired annotation generators, the old schema checker, steps.md exporter, per-set booklet maps and systems/mechanisms/preview sidecars were removed. Generated training splits are no longer tracked. Optional 3D references for unfinished sets remain. Local annotation folders and removed files are preserved in the external cleanup backup, not treated as new training examples.

Several older sets and MOCs have inventory differences or uncertain part identifications. The training packer excludes every set marked needs review. Passing the automated checks does not establish full mechanical correctness.
